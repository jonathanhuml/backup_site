About

Howdy! I'm an engineer working on brain-computer interfaces at Zyphra in downtown San Francisco.

Originally, I'm from Durham, North Carolina.

I studied Mathematics (B.A.) and Statistics (B.S.) at UNC-Chapel Hill, where I began my research in computer vision for autonomous vehicles.

I then moved to Boston for the Computational Engineering program at Harvard University, conducting research in the CRISP Group where I developed a passion for computational neuroscience.
